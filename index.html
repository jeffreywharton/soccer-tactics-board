The 3-3-4 with a stopper

Three strikers, three midfielders, three defenders and a stopper in front of them (written back-to-front it's a 3-1-3-3). Move the ball and watch how every player should shift. Your team attacks up the field toward the orange goal and defends the blue goal at the bottom.

Ball control

Touch or drag anywhere on the field and the ball goes exactly there. Tap a player to see their job.

Rules the whole team follows

Shift with the ball

All ten field players slide left, right, up and down as a group. Nobody stays glued to a spot.

Wide when we have it

Wingers hug the touchline and the back three spread out, so the field gets as big as possible.

Narrow when they have it

Players far from the ball tuck toward the middle. Close the center first; the far touchline can wait.

Keep it short

Aim for 35–45 m from the back line to the strikers. Bigger gaps are where the other team plays through you.

Follow every shot

When we shoot, the strikers who didn't shoot crash the goal for the rebound. Keepers this age drop a lot of balls.

Stopper steps, sweeper covers

When the stopper steps up to the ball, the center back drops in behind and the outside defenders pinch in.
